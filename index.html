Frequently Asked Questions

Are Soji sauces gluten-free?

Yes, all Soji sauces are crafted to be gluten-free, ensuring they are safe for those with gluten sensitivities or celiac disease.

How should I store Soji sauces?

For optimal freshness, store Soji sauces in a cool, dry place before opening. After opening, refrigerate and use within one month for the best flavor.

Can I use Soji sauces in cooking?

Absolutely! Soji sauces are versatile and can be used as marinades, dipping sauces, or flavor enhancers in various dishes.

How spicy is Soji?

Spice levels vary by country:
Korea: deep, fermented heat
Thailand: bright, fast, balanced spice
Japan: umami-forward and subtle
